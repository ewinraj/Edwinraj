About

I am a mathematics educator with 17 years of experience across Arts & Science colleges, engineering colleges and a deemed university, plus two years in industry. My PhD work was on the Hyers–Ulam stability of quadratic and mixed type functional equations. Today my research also covers data analytics and machine learning, including student retention models and healthcare analytics.

Alongside teaching, I work on curriculum design, outcome-based education, examinations and institutional rankings.
